A worker turn that completes past its budget keeps its committed result. The drive ends at the turn boundary instead of throwing `supervisor budget exhausted`, and the node settles `down` with that result (`outRef`), reason `budget stop: ...`, and a `budgetViolation` naming each channel with the amount reserved and the amount spent. The manager's wake also carries `budgetStop` (`tokens 235,857 of 42,000`). Readers that split on `done` therefore never count budget-stopped work as finished. `BudgetOverspend.channel` gains `deadline` (time given and time taken, in ms) for a child that settled past its time box. The pre-turn refusal names the spent channels too. Before, the Lab's limits audit counted 28 such worker turns (26.77 turn-hours) metered and then thrown away.
