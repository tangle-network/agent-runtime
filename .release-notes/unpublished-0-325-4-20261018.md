0.325.4 was tagged but not published: it held a retained stream that owed its terminal frame open for five seconds after the exact result, and the retained-provider laws require that an exact result wakes the turn at once on a quiet stream. 0.325.5 carries every 0.325.4 change with that rule corrected: after its exact result, a stream reads on only while its frames keep arriving, each within 100 ms of the last, and a quiet stream closes on the result at once.
