A manager whose completion check accepted its `submit_result` now settles as soon as the turn that submitted it has ended and its usage is metered. Its end-of-turn workspace capture no longer holds the settlement: with a workspace retention port that has `captureNative`, a retained owner hands that capture to its scope owner, which awaits it before the environment's next turn and before the environment is destroyed, and journals its receipt as `execution-evidence`. A deadline or abort that arrives while an accepted turn is still ending waits for its result, for at most 5 minutes, instead of settling the node `down`: `Executor.resultAccepted()` and `Agent.resultAccepted()` report the acceptance, and the root deadline gives an accepted root the same grace. A nested manager releases the environment it kept between its turns once its own scope finishes, and the run settlement waits for every manager's capture and release under either `retainedAtSettlement` policy. At that release a verified capture recorded as evidence counts as the turn's receipt, and a checkpoint request the provider answers `not_found` for more than 20 minutes after it was made no longer keeps its source environment running. Each workspace capture journals a `workspace-capture` record with its queue wait, capture time and archive bytes. On Discovery Lab run terraform-economics-20261005f, five nodes with accepted results were settled `down` by deadlines that fell while their turns waited 4 to 28 minutes on the shared capture queue, and each root's sandbox stayed up after its run settled.
