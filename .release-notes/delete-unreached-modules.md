Removed public helpers that no consumer reaches. A search of every tangle-network checkout on the build hosts (about 45 repositories, including braid, supervisor-lab, discovery-lab and the product agents) found no import of any of them. From `./kernel`: `auditIntent`, `defaultAuditorInstruction` and the `AuditIntentInput`, `AuditIntentOptions` and `IntentAudit` types. From `./mcp`: `detectExecutor` and `DetectExecutorArgs`; the MCP bin stopped calling it some time ago. From `./profiles`: the UI-audit workspace writer (`appendFindings`, `initAuditWorkspace`, `readAuditRegistry`, `registerCaptures`, `summarizeRegistry`, `writeAuditIndex`, and the `AppendFindingsResult`, `AuditIndex`, `AuditRegistry`, `AuditRegistryCapture` and `RegisterCapturesOptions` types), plus `createUiAuditorValidator`, `encodeAuditTaskEnvelope`, `decodeAuditTaskEnvelope` and `formatAuditorPrompt`. The UI-audit data types, `parseAuditorEvents`, the lens prompts and `delegate_ui_audit` are unchanged.
