A root driver now survives an infrastructure outage of up to 15 minutes. A transient failure in which nothing ran (no tokens spent, no child settled, no progress), such as a Platform 502 during a restart or a lost provider transport, no longer consumes `driverRetry.maxAttempts` or `maxConsecutiveFailures` until its streak has lasted `driverRetry.transientOutageMs` (default 900000). The run's deadline, budget, and cancellation still bound it, terminal failures still stop at once, and a transient failure that spent tokens keeps the existing bounds. Every failure backoff and unavailable pause is now jittered over the upper half of its value. Before this, Discovery run research-nqs-20261003system2 settled `driver-failed` after 12 attempts in the first two minutes of a five-minute Platform restart.
