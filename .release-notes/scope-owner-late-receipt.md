A scope owner whose materialization was finalized `unknown` (its attempt ended before its agent reported) now keeps running when its report arrives later, on the same attempt or a later one. The `unknown` record attests no backend, model, plan or execution identity, so a later report cannot have changed one; the committed record stays `unknown`, and an attempt not yet bound binds its transport to it. Before, the report was refused as `scope owner materialization changed mid-run; these fields must match across driver attempts but differ: status`, classified terminal: on Discovery from 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-05, 20 nodes were killed this way after 5 to 96 minutes of work, including wait-cost-smoke-20261005b-wake on 0.301.0, whose retained owner's result-time report lost the race to its scope's finalization.
